Guide & Help

Quick answers to how this tool works, and how it supports different needs.

How to record a throw

  1. Go to Field View.
  2. Type the athlete's name, or leave it blank and one will be filled in automatically (e.g. "Athlete 1").
  3. Type the throw distance in metres, e.g. 14.20, or use the − / + buttons to adjust it.
  4. Select Record Throw.
  5. The shot leaves a trail as it travels and settles exactly on the landing distance, while the scoreboard below shows the exact number.

What the distance means

The distance is how far the shot travelled from the front edge of the throwing circle to where it landed, measured in metres. This matches how distances are measured in real shot put competitions.

Switching between views

Use the Field View / Results View toggle near the top of either page, or the main navigation bar at the top of every page. The current page is always shown clearly in both. Results now shows a leaderboard ranking every athlete by their best throw, with the full throw-by-throw log underneath.

Accessibility features in this tool

  • Every throw result is shown as plain text, not just as an animation.
  • High-contrast text and colours throughout.
  • Large, clearly labelled buttons and form fields.
  • Visible focus outlines for anyone navigating with a keyboard.
  • A "Skip to main content" link for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • Error messages describe exactly what to fix, not just "invalid".
  • Colour is never the only way information is shown — e.g. a personal best is also labelled "Personal best" in text.
  • If your device requests reduced motion, a note says so and the shot appears directly at its landing spot instead of animating.

Shot put basics

In shot put, an athlete throws ("puts") a heavy metal ball from inside a marked circle. The throw is measured from the front edge of the circle to the point where the shot first lands, and the furthest valid throw counts as the athlete's result.

For coaches, athletes and spectators

  • Athletes: use Field View to log throws during practice and track your personal best on the Results page.
  • Coaches: use the Results page for a quick, readable summary during a session.
  • Spectators: Field View gives a simple visual sense of distance, with the exact number always shown in text too.
